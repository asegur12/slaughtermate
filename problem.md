# Slaughtermate

## Problem

A client who enjoys chess and hack'n'slash games wants a game that combines both:
chess for the look, hack'n'slash for the gameplay. The player gains weapons and
spells and fights waves of enemies and bosses.

## Initial requirements

- **R1** The player is a white chess pawn, enemies are black chess pieces, and bosses are giant checkered pieces.
- **R2** The game has a large map with structures such as cities or castles.
- **R3** Large swarms of enemies spawn on the outskirts of the map and come toward the player from all sides.
- **R4** Each enemy piece type has its own movement and attacks.
- **R5** There are 6 bosses, one giant piece of each kind. Each one ends a wave and must be killed before the next wave begins.
- **R6** The player is promoted through the other 5 pieces as the waves go on.
- **R7** The player picks 1 of 4 weapon classes per run: Sword (melee slashes, spin slash charged attack), Bow (ranged shots, big arrow charged attack), Staff (ranged area attacks, big magic blast charged attack), Hammer (melee area attacks, giant slam charged attack).
- **R8** Spells come in 5 rarity tiers: tiers 1–2 are small-scale, tiers 3–4 deal large damage in larger areas, and tier 5 deals massive damage over massive areas.
- **R9** Each tier has 5 to 8 spells, and the player can equip 3 spells at once.
- **R10** The highest-tier spells include sun, moon, darkness, light, tech and hyper types.
- **R11** Enemies scale up in HP and strength with each level gained and can kill the player in the late game.
- **R12** Rewards are random but fair, so every spell has a fair chance of being offered.
- **R13** A shop sells spells and other items.
- **R14** Controls: W A S D move, left click is a basic attack aimed at the mouse, holding and releasing left click is a charged attack, and 1, 2, 3 cast the equipped spells.
- **R15** Before playing, the menu shows a scoreboard with each run's highest floor and score.
- **R16** The game runs fully offline, with no internet connection.
